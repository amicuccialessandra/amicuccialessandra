## Hi, I'm Alessandra <br/>
_Economics & Policy Evaluation | Data Analysis | Coding for Impact_ <br/>

I'm an Economics graduate from Sapienza University of Rome, interested in international trade, development, and political economy, with the goal of pursuing a PhD in Economics. My research experience includes traineeships at the European Central Bank and the Kiel Institute, and a research assistantship at Sapienza on a project commissioned by IFAD. <br/>

🔧 I work with R, Python, Stata, and MATLAB to construct datasets, conduct empirical analysis, and develop research tools. <br/>
🌍 I enjoy working in international teams and speak Italian 🇮🇹, English 🇬🇧, and Spanish 🇪🇸. <br/>
📊 My master's thesis explored maritime trade exposure and conflict in Nigeria, combining trade indicators, georeferenced conflict events, and satellite data. <br/>
🔍 I value clear code, reproducible analysis, and research that helps explain economic and social outcomes. <br/>

_Always curious. Always coding_ <br/>
